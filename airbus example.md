## July 2026: Airbus Moves {.center}

Airbus migrates **70 critical applications** from AWS to Scaleway, 
the French cloud from the Iliad group.

::: fragment
The reason is not price. It's not features.
:::

::: fragment
Airbus's cloud tender **scored bidders on their protection 
against the CLOUD Act**. As a scored procurement criterion, 
like price or uptime. 15% of the total score, reportedly.
:::

::: fragment

Scaleway could, by existing.
:::
