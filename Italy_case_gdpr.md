## When Law Fails, Architecture Steps In {.center}

::: notes
The plot twist: maybe you believe regulators will fix this. 
Here is what trying looks like.
:::

- *November 2024*: **Italy**'s data protection authority **fines OpenAI** €15 million. The first GDPR fine against a chatbot maker, ever.

::: fragment
- *March 2026*: the Court of Rome **annuls** it. OpenAI's Irish subsidiary makes **Ireland the lead regulator** under GDPR's one-stop-shop rule. Italy never had jurisdiction. The substance was **never judged**.
:::

::: fragment
> The enforcement mechanism itself became the escape hatch.
:::

::: notes
Keep it brisk, it's one beat. The absurdism: the rule designed
to make enforcement simpler across Europe ended up voiding the
only fine Europe had landed. If someone asks about the
Irish DPC follow-up: the point stands regardless of what Ireland
does later, because the years-long gap is the vulnerability.
:::
