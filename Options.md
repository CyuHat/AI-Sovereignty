## The Map, Written Down

**Zone 1, self-hosted.** vLLM, Ollama, llama.cpp. Absolute control,
your hardware. Zero data leaves.

**Zone 2, full-stack European clouds.** OVHcloud, Scaleway, STACKIT,
T Cloud Public, OUTSCALE, Cloud Temple, NumSpot, IONOS. European owners,
European stacks.

**Zone 3, GPU neoclouds.** Verda, Nebius, Nscale, CloudFerro. European
or hybrid compute, bring your own models. Jurisdiction varies.

**Zone 4, routers and gateways.** cortecs, TextCortex, Requesty. One
European legal wrapper, many models underneath.

**Zone 5, wrapped hyperscalers.** S3NS, Bleu, AWS European Sovereign
Cloud, Azure EU Data Boundary. A European mask over American machinery.
